Word Cash loyalty and cashout experience.

Product scope

What the app is

The product is a first-party mobile game hub. Players browse lightweight games, track loyalty progress, and use a cashout entry point when an available balance is ready for partner fulfillment.

!

Loyalty Points are shown as progress toward the current cycle. The cashout module appears as a separate action when a user has an available unclaimed balance.

User-facing components

  • Home hub with unclaimed balance, cycle timer, loyalty progress, and game catalog.
  • Game tiles with generic earn labels such as per game, per puzzle, or per level.
  • Optional store for gameplay currency and time-limited Gold Club benefits.
  • User-initiated cashout flow handled through the partner SDK or API.
  • Lightweight non-blocking loyalty notifications after qualifying play events.

Experience rules

  • Loyalty progress, timer status, and cashout state are visually distinct.
  • Game tiles use ordinary puzzle, word, card, and grid-game presentation.
  • Store purchases support gameplay currency and Gold Club timing only.
  • Reward provider options, redemption terms, and fulfillment status remain partner-controlled.
  • Production copy and edge states should be approved before launch.

User journey

Cashout flow

The experience is structured so the player can see progress, open the cashout entry point, choose a provider, and complete fulfillment through the partner-controlled flow.

Open Home

The player lands on the hub and sees current progress, an available balance area, a cycle timer, and game options.

Play Games

The player chooses a game. Loyalty notifications may appear after qualifying events without blocking gameplay.

Choose Convert

When a balance is available, the player may open the cashout entry point and select a provider from the available options.

Partner Fulfillment

Provider selection, required account details, confirmation, and fulfillment status are handled by the partner API or SDK.

Integration boundaries

Responsibilities

This structure keeps the app UI, loyalty progress layer, and partner fulfillment flow separated for clearer implementation ownership.

App UI Owns

  • Home hub layout and navigation.
  • Loyalty progress display and cycle status.
  • Safe game catalog presentation.
  • Entry point into cashout.
  • Post-conversion success or return state.

Partner Flow Owns

  • Provider catalog availability.
  • Provider terms, restrictions, and redemption instructions.
  • Partner-hosted selection or confirmation UI.
  • Fulfillment status and transaction identifiers.
  • Partner-required disclosures and support content.

Shared Review Owns

  • Approved user-facing copy.
  • Privacy and data minimization requirements.
  • Age, geography, and eligibility constraints where applicable.
  • Error states for unavailable providers or failed fulfillment.
  • Launch review across app, store, and partner surfaces.
Suggested copy

User language set

The language should be plain and operational. It should explain what the user can see, what action is available, and where partner terms apply.

Home hub

Play participating games to build loyalty progress during the active cycle. Available cashout options may vary by account, location, and partner terms.

Cashout entry

Cash out your available balance through a supported provider. Availability and redemption terms are provided by the selected partner.

Store

Gem packages support gameplay. Eligible packages may include a time-limited Gold Club benefit. Cashout availability is shown separately in the cashout module.

Notification

Loyalty progress updated. Keep playing participating games to continue building progress during the current cycle.

Use

  • Available balance
  • Loyalty progress
  • Participating games
  • Partner terms apply
  • May vary by account, location, and availability

Reserve for internal docs

  • Backend calculation details
  • Cycle-level reconciliation
  • Forecasting and optimization assumptions
  • Eligibility scoring and fraud signals
  • Partner settlement and operational economics
Launch notes

Open implementation items

The items below should be resolved with the owning product, engineering, and partner teams before the experience moves into production.

Before integration approval

  • Confirm partner requirements for provider display, expiration, disclosures, and support.
  • Confirm whether eligibility gates are required by age, state, device, or account status.
  • Confirm the exact copy for unavailable providers, failed cashout, and reversed issuance.
  • Confirm privacy and data-sharing disclosures for all partner API or SDK events.

What remains internal

  • Calculation logic for balances, goals, and cycle updates.
  • Ad monetization attribution, projections, and reconciliation.
  • Fraud, abuse, and eligibility scoring.
  • Operational economics and partner settlement details.
